Start here: what this site is

The Award Hacker is for the person who spends $2,000–$4,000 a month on a credit card, has heard friends talk about "flying free with points," and has never earned a single mile. We explain points like a friendly mechanic: which currency to earn first, which card rules actually matter, how to search for award seats without losing your mind, and what every ticket really costs out of pocket.

Our rule is simple: show the math or don't say it. Annual fees are stated upfront, bonus terms are qualified, and when we earn a commission on a link, we say so in plain language right next to it.
